Guidelines

  • Transactions
    1 point is awarded per 100 LKR spent. Points apply instantly to the customer's balance.

    Customers move up automatically: Regular → Coffee Enthusiast → Coffee Connoisseur, based on lifetime points.

    New rewards appear on the customer app immediately after creation.
